Overview & Privacy Commitment

Welcome to Resume Brain ("we," "our," or "us"), an intelligent career-document creation, tailoring, and ATS optimization mobile application developed and operated by VALIXIS.

We believe personal career documents contain some of an individual's most sensitive information—including employment history, educational credentials, contact numbers, home addresses, compensation markers, and proprietary project details. For this reason, Resume Brain is built upon the foundational principle of Zero-Trust Local-First Privacy.

✅

Core Guarantee

We do not operate remote databases that aggregate your resumes. We do not sell your personal data to recruiters, data brokers, or advertisers. Your documents remain under your absolute physical and digital ownership.

Zero-Tracking & Zero-Telemetry Guarantee

Unlike conventional web and mobile applications, Resume Brain enforces strict telemetry isolation:

  • No Analytics SDKs: We do not embed Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, Segment, Flurry, or Amplitude.
  • No Crash Telemetry Uploads: No external crash reporting frameworks (such as Firebase Crashlytics or Sentry) are configured to collect or transmit device memory dumps or stack traces to external servers.
  • No Advertising Identifiers: We do not read, store, or monetize Google Advertising IDs (AAID), IDFA, or hardware serial numbers.
  • No Background Network Polling: The application does not maintain persistent background network connections to harvest telemetry.

AI Processing Architecture & Transparency

Resume Brain incorporates a flexible, hybrid AI engine designed for maximal privacy and control:

A. Offline Staging / Heuristic Engine

By default, resume parsing, ATS scoring, keyword frequency extraction, and rule-based grammar enhancements run on the local VALIXIS On-Device Engine. This engine executes 100% locally on your smartphone processor without consuming internet bandwidth or transmitting a single character outside your device.

B. Optional Cloud AI Services (Google Gemini API)

When you choose to enable advanced generative features (such as deep bullet point rewrites, dynamic role tailoring, or custom career summarization):

  • Direct Secure Transport: Requests are transmitted over encrypted TLS 1.3 connections directly between your device and Google's official Gemini API endpoints.
  • Strict Scope: Only the specific excerpt or text block you request to optimize (along with target role context) is included in the API prompt.
  • Zero Training Retention: In accordance with Google Cloud and Gemini API enterprise terms, prompts submitted via paid or API keys are not used to train or refine public foundation models.
  • Bring Your Own Key (BYOK) Support: You can enter your own personal Gemini API key. Keys are encrypted on your device using Android Keystore and never sent to VALIXIS servers.
💡

Full Offline Freedom

You can turn off Cloud AI features at any time in Settings → AI Engine Configuration to operate Resume Brain completely offline.

6

Local Storage & Hardware-Backed Encryption

We implement multi-layered defense to safeguard your resumes locally:

  • Hive Database Sandboxing: All structured resumes and styling parameters reside in local Hive binary storage (resumes_box and settings_box), accessible solely by the Resume Brain application sandbox.
  • Hardware Keystore Integration: Sensitive parameters, including master access PINs and optional AI credentials, are managed via flutter_secure_storage, utilizing Android KeyStore cryptography.
  • PDF Generation in Memory: Resume PDFs are rendered in local memory. When you export or print, documents are saved directly to your chosen local folder or passed directly to Android's native system print spooler.
7

Cloud Sync & Optional Multi-Device Services

If you choose to activate optional cloud sync features (such as multi-device synchronization via Supabase backend services):

  • Opt-In Only: Cloud sync is entirely optional and is never turned on without your direct consent.
  • Row-Level Security (RLS): Data synchronized to cloud storage is protected by strict user-level authentication barriers and Row-Level Security policies.
  • Data Minimization: Only your saved resume objects are backed up; we never sync unnecessary telemetry or local file system metadata.

Security Safeguards & Privacy Lock

Resume Brain provides built-in application-level security mechanisms to defend against physical snooping and unauthorized local access:

  • Privacy Lock Overlay: When enabled, Resume Brain automatically conceals resume content and requires biometric authentication or PIN entry upon app launch or returning from the background.
  • Auto-Lock Inactivity Timer: You can configure custom lock timers (Immediate, 1 min, 5 min) to automatically secure the workspace when idle.
  • Encrypted PDF Exports: Exported PDF documents can be protected with standard document passwords for safe emailing and archiving.

Your Global Privacy Rights (GDPR, CCPA, CPRA, LGPD)

Regardless of where you reside, we grant all users complete autonomy over their personal data in accordance with international privacy regulations:

  • Right to Access: You can view all saved information directly within the app's visual editors at any time.
  • Right to Rectification: You can edit, update, or overwrite any resume section or profile field instantaneously.
  • Right to Data Portability: You can export your data as formatted PDF resumes or structured JSON files for transfer to other platforms.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You can permanently wipe any resume or all application data instantly.
  • Right to Opt-Out: You can disable cloud AI and network features to ensure zero data ever leaves your device.

Data Deletion & Retention Procedures

Because VALIXIS does not store your resumes on centralized servers, you do not need to submit a formal data deletion ticket to have your data erased. You have instant self-service deletion powers:

🗑️

Instant 1-Tap Permanent Deletion

Option A (In-App): Tap the delete icon on any resume card to permanently remove it from your device.
Option B (System Level): Open your device's Settings → Apps → Resume Brain → Storage → Clear Data.
Option C (Uninstall): Uninstalling Resume Brain from your device permanently purges all local Hive databases, caches, and Keystore keys.

Children's Privacy Protection (COPPA / Age Limits)

We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children. If a parent or guardian becomes aware that a child has entered personal information into the app on their device, the data can be instantly wiped by clearing the application storage or uninstalling the app.
