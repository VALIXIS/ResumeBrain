Resume Brain is built with privacy by design. All resume data, personal details, and career information are stored locally on your device. We do not upload your resumes to external servers, and we do not use tracking APIs.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Resume Brain ("we," "our," "us"), a mobile application developed by VALIXIS. Resume Brain is an AI-powered career-document application designed to help users create, edit, tailor, and analyze professional resumes and curriculum vitae (CVs).

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data you process while using our application. This Privacy Policy describes how Resume Brain processes, stores, and handles your information, and explains the strict controls in place to guarantee your data security.

2. Developer Information

Resume Brain is owned and operated by:

  • Developer / Company: VALIXIS
  • Application Package Name: com.valixis.resumebrain
  • Platform: Google Play Store (Android)

3. Information You Provide

Resume Brain processes the information you manually type or import into the application to compile your resume. This career-related data is under your absolute control and includes:

  • Personal Information: Full name, job title, email address, phone number, physical location/address, and personal website or portfolio links.
  • Professional Profile: Professional summary statements and career objectives.
  • Employment History: Company names, job titles, locations, employment dates, and descriptive bullet points detailing your responsibilities and accomplishments.
  • Education History: Institution names, degrees earned, fields of study, locations, attendance dates, and Grade Point Averages (GPA).
  • Projects & Skills: Project names, roles, descriptions, technologies used, links, skill lists, and skill proficiency levels.
  • Certifications & Languages: Certification names, issuing organizations, dates, credentials URLs, languages spoken, and proficiency levels.
  • Social Links: Platform handles and links (e.g., LinkedIn, GitHub).
  • Job Descriptions: Text from target job postings that you paste into the app to evaluate resume matching.

4. Information Collected Automatically

Unlike many modern applications, Resume Brain does not track you. We do not automatically collect or transmit any of the following to remote servers:

  • No Analytics: No tracking SDKs (such as Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, or Amplitude) are installed in the application.
  • No Crash Reporting: The application does not contain crash reporting libraries (such as Firebase Crashlytics or Sentry). No logs or diagnostics are transmitted off-device.
  • No Advertising Identifiers: We do not collect or monitor Google Advertising IDs (AAID), IP addresses, or device fingerprints.
  • No Accounts: You do not need to register, create an account, or log in to use Resume Brain. Consequently, we do not store credentials, profile pictures, or registration details online.

5. How Resume and Career Data Is Processed

Any processing of your resume data occurs entirely locally on your device:

  • PDF Export and Printing: When you generate a PDF of your resume, the PDF bytes are compiled locally using the system font engine and the device's native PDF generation libraries. The resulting document is stored in your device's temporary memory or saved directly to your local file system according to your instructions.
  • Local Sharing: When you choose to share your resume, the application initiates a native system share dialog using the device's default sharing controller. Resume Brain does not host, upload, or control the transfer of shared files.

6. AI-Powered Features

Resume Brain features several AI-assisted modules, including professional text improvement, resume analysis, ATS scoring, and job matching. To guarantee complete privacy:

  • Local Simulation: All AI features in this version of the application run on an offline, locally simulated staging engine (the VALIXIS Staging Engine).
  • No Remote Transmission: Your resume contents, personal details, professional descriptions, and target job descriptions are never transmitted over the internet to external AI providers (such as Google Gemini, OpenAI, or Microsoft Azure).
  • Local Output: All suggestions, scoring metrics, and rewrites are generated on-device, ensuring that your sensitive professional details remain completely confidential.

7. Data Storage and Retention

Your data is stored securely using the following offline mechanisms:

  • Hive Local Database: Resumes and settings are stored locally on your device's internal storage in key-value database boxes managed by the Hive library (specifically resumes_box and settings_box). This data is isolated within the sandboxed storage allocated to Resume Brain by the Android operating system.
  • Data Retention: Since VALIXIS does not host or operate a remote database, we do not retain your data. Your resumes and settings remain on your device indefinitely until you choose to delete them.

8. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do not sell, trade, rent, or share your personal data. Because Resume Brain is an offline utility app, there are no third-party APIs, backends (such as Firebase or Supabase), or advertiser SDKs integrated into the code. No third-party entity has access to the information you enter into the application.

9. File and Device Permissions

To provide core resume-building services, the application requests the following permissions from the operating system, which are used strictly for local operations:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required by the Flutter framework to enable core network connectivity for web font rendering. No user data is sent over this connection.
  • android.permission.READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE: Used to allow you to import files or select template assets locally on your device. (Max SDK version 32).
  • android.permission.WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE: Used to export, print, and save generated PDF resumes to your device's downloads or documents folder. (Max SDK version 28).

10. Data Security

Since your resume data is stored locally on your device, the security of your data depends heavily on the security of your device. We encourage you to secure your phone with strong PINs/passwords, device encryption, and biometric locks to prevent unauthorized physical access to your local data. While we implement standard local sandboxing to isolate our database boxes, no offline storage is completely immune to custom device exploits or root-level compromises.

11. Children's Privacy

Our services are not designed for or targeted at children under the age of 13. Because Resume Brain does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data remotely, we do not knowingly collect or solicit any information from children under 13 years of age. If you believe a child has entered personal data on a device, it can be deleted immediately by clearing the app's cache or uninstalling the app.

12. Your Rights and Data Control

Under local privacy laws (including GDPR, CCPA, and others), you maintain complete control over your data. In Resume Brain, you can exercise these rights instantly without contacting us:

  • Access and View: You can view all saved resume information directly on the application's editor screens.
  • Rectification: You can edit any details in your resumes at any time.
  • Deletion / Erasure: You can delete any resume from the app dashboard using the "Delete" action. To erase all data, profiles, and settings permanently, navigate to your Android device settings, select Apps → Resume Brain → Storage, and tap Clear Data. Alternatively, uninstalling the application will wipe all local Hive databases.

13. Third-Party Links

The application may generate resumes that include links to your social profiles or personal websites. The app itself does not link to third-party services. If you share your resume with others, they may access any links you have chose to write in your document. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites linked in your resume.

We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about our data practices.
